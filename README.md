# CAIR Austin Then & Now · 2017–2026

Independent source-first documentary archive of CAIR-Austin public material from the chapter's 2017 launch through 2026.

## Run
Open `index.html` locally or deploy the folder directly to Vercel/GitHub Pages.

## Editorial standard
- No CAIR logos or official-site imitation.
- Separate documented evidence from research targets.
- Do not treat related terms as legally or factually identical.
- Link claims to primary/current or archived sources.
- The Austin chapter launch baseline is CAIR National's May 4, 2017 announcement, which states the inaugural Austin banquet occurred April 29, 2017.
