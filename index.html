METHODOLOGY

What counts as a “change”?

Added. A concept or named issue appears where the sampled earlier record did not show it. This label is used cautiously.

Renamed. The underlying activity appears continuous but the public vocabulary changes.

Expanded. A previously documented activity becomes more explicit, broader, more prominent, or receives its own program/category.

De-emphasized. A formerly prominent issue becomes less visible in sampled public-facing material.

Unchanged / continuous. The core activity remains clearly documented across periods even if examples or wording evolve.

Research target. No conclusion yet. The year is present so gaps are visible rather than silently omitted.
